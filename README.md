## About

I build products end to end, with 5 years of experience across mobile and web.

Right now I'm the technical owner of a fintech card app in production: I created the
repository, defined its architecture, sign **90%+ of its commits** and have shipped
**9 releases** to the App Store and Play Store. I designed the white-label architecture
that builds three different branded apps from a single codebase, and wrote two custom
**Expo Modules** in Swift and Kotlin — native wallet card provisioning, and biometric
login backed by a key pair generated inside the device's secure element.

I work across the whole stack: native iOS/Android modules, React Native, Next.js, and
NestJS + PostgreSQL on the backend — usually in regulated domains where correctness
matters (identity verification, compliance, money movement).
